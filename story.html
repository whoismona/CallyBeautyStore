OUR STORY

From One Wig to Two Shops, The Journey Continues.

Where It All Began

Cally-Hair Beauty Store began in 2017, while I was still a student. What started with the sale of just one wig gradually grew into something much bigger. With consistency, passion and a commitment to quality, I started receiving bulk orders and realized that this could become more than just a small business.

After graduation, I rented a one-bedroom apartment and started producing blend hairs from scratch. Everything was done from home, from ventilation to wigging and styling.

Taking the Next Step

As the business continued to grow, so did the needs of our customers. In 2021, I opened my first walk-in store.

Customers also began requesting ready-made factory wigs, so I expanded the business to include factory-made wigs alongside our ventilation services.

Where We Are Today

Today, what started with one wig in 2017 has grown into two shops. The journey from producing wigs at home to operating multiple walk-in locations is a reminder of how far consistency, hard work and customer support can take a dream.

We remain committed to providing quality human hair, ready-made factory wigs and professional ventilation services while continuing to grow and serve our customers better.
